# Game audio credits

All audio files in this directory are released under CC0 / Public Domain by their creators.

- `flowerbed-fields-06.mp3` — “Flowerbed Fields [Loop]”, by Zane Little Music. Converted from the WAV source with amplitude at 6% so the background level also applies on iOS; device volume controls still adjust playback. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/flowerbed-fields-loop
- `claw-drop-soft.wav`, `claw-close-soft.wav`, `prize-rattle-soft.wav` — mechanical effects by BMacZero, from “Mechanical Sounds”. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/mechanical-sounds
- `coin-insert-soft.wav` — “vending_machine_coin_insert.wav”, by Krokulator. Converted from the high-quality MP3 preview of the CC0 recording. Source: https://freesound.org/people/Krokulator/sounds/696745/
- `clerk-call-soft.wav` — “Pleasing Bell Sound Effect”, by Spring Spring. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/pleasing-bell-sound-effect
- `title-unlock-soft.wav` (`level_up.wav`), `participation-soft.wav` (`level_passed.wav`), `memorial-soft.wav` (`fail_2.wav`), `clerk-arrival-soft.wav` (`pick_up.wav`), `clerk-judgment-soft.wav` (`click_2.wav`) — from “Chiptune SFX Pack” by FrogPog. Source: https://opengameart.org/content/chiptune-sfx-pack

The effect files are attenuated to 75% of their previous amplitude so the 25% reduction also applies in iOS Safari, where media-element volume settings may be ignored. The Web Audio claw motor uses the same reduction.
